COASTLINE: ENDLESS DRIVE

An original Nintendo DS procedural highway game created for this project.
Original vehicle and roadside illustrations were generated for Coastline and
prepared as palette-indexed sprites and textures with RGB555 palettes. Road geometry, driving, traffic, scoring,
progression, dashboard, menus, synthesized music and engine/road/horn sounds
were created for the game. The Fusion, Pearl, Voltage, Alloy, Glacier and Bloom interfaces and
departure-themed garage interiors use original code and existing local textures.
The dual-screen opening, sky animation and profile selector are original code. The requested vehicle names identify the new original vehicle illustrations;
no commercial game art or ROM data are used. Checked-in source art permits rebuilding without an image service.

Free Roam's fixed street network, map, traffic and city renderer are original
code. Its detailed overhead vehicle paintings and coastal scenery are original
image-generated artwork, with reviewed material masks and steered axle poses.
Its coastal buildings, garden objects and lawn use new original generated artwork. No GTA assets or commercial ROM data are used.

Text uses DejaVu Sans/Serif. Font license: licenses/DejaVu.txt.
Native platform: libnds, devkitARM startup/linker files, newlib and GCC runtime.
libnds license: licenses/libnds.txt. Pinned source revisions and installation
steps are in tools/setup_nds.sh and platform/build_sdk.py. The established
Nintendo DS platform boundary was reused from this repository's Asterwild
project; driving gameplay, procedural world and interface are separate.

Delta is the intended phone frontend. Validation here uses a native Nintendo
DS emulator. No physical iPhone or DS testing is claimed.
