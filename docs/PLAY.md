# Playing Bramblebound: First Light

Open `Bramblebound.gba` in your GBA emulator. No patching or base game ROM is needed. Keyboard mappings depend on your emulator; the controls below refer to GBA buttons.

| Button | Action |
| --- | --- |
| D-pad | Walk / select |
| A | Talk, inspect, confirm, advance dialogue |
| B | Run while walking; back out of menus |
| Start | Open the journal/menu |
| Select | Choose or revise the selected companion's growth path in COMPANIONS |
| L / R | Change FIELD GUIDE pages |

From the main wild-battle menu, B leaves safely. Inside a submenu, B returns to the battle menu. Rivals cannot be recruited.

Your first objective is to talk to Mara in the stone square north of where you start. The bottom of the screen always gives the next objective. Characters and signs explain the rest.

## Useful basics

- NUDGE is a free attack. Signature attacks cost energy.
- SETTLE restores health and energy; it also gives the opponent a turn.
- SOOTHE prepares a wild creature for recruitment. After choosing a growth path, that move becomes BURST or SHELTER, so use the half-health route instead—or travel with a friend who still knows SOOTHE. Luma's GLIMMER also calms creatures.
- BEFRIEND always succeeds with one treat when the creature has half health or less, or is calm. Trying too early wastes no treat and no turn.
- LEAF beats TIDE; TIDE beats EMBER; EMBER beats LEAF. LEAF and TIDE are also useful against STONE.
- Everyone traveling with you earns experience. A creature helping Nell in town is resting instead; talk to Nell to recall it.
- At level 3, choose STRIDER for offense or WARDEN for recovery and defense. You can revise a path at camp or in Mossbell without losing levels.
- Rest at the trail lantern, or speak to Mara/Eli, for free recovery. Mara and Tavi refill low treat supplies; Eli replenishes low Tonic supplies.
- Losing a battle returns you safely to Mossbell. Your friends and inventory remain yours.

## Saving

Use START > SAVE JOURNEY. Important milestones and area transitions also save automatically. The title screen offers CONTINUE when a valid save exists. Choosing NEW JOURNEY asks for confirmation before replacing progress.

This ROM uses 32 KB SRAM battery saves. Automatic save-type detection should recognize `SRAM_V113`; if your emulator needs a manual save-type setting, choose SRAM / 32 KB. Keep the emulator's `.sav` file. Let the emulator finish flushing its save before copying or closing it. Emulator save states are separate from the game's own save system.

## Where this draft ends

Iris waits at the northeast lookout across the bridge. Meet a second companion first. Your team is healed before the duel. Winning awards the first Trail Pin and completes this opening. You can keep exploring, try another growth path, help the garden, and save afterward.
